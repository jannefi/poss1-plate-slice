# WCSFIX guard — a fit that reports `ok` and is wrong across the tile

**Status (2026-09-09):** defect found, sized survey-wide, documented in both S0
release READMEs; derived product S1 re-cut without the affected tiles. The
guard itself (last section) is being implemented; until it lands, the check
in "How to find such a tile" is the way to audit a build.

## What happened

The pipeline refits each tile's astrometry against Gaia before the vetoes
(`vasco/wcsfix_early.py`): detections are matched to proper-motion-propagated
Gaia within 5″, and a degree-2 polynomial per axis is fitted to the offsets
with σ-clipping. On a healthy tile that is ~3,000 tie points fitted to
σ ≈ 0.13″. The fit's own diagnostics — tie points, σ on the kept points — are
written to `catalogs/wcsfix_status.json` with `ok: true` whenever the fit
converged with at least 20 tie points.

On `tile_RA24.686_DECp33.529` (plate XE296, a corner tile) the raw plate
solution is poor enough that the 5″ bootstrap found only **312** tie points
(survey 1st percentile: 928), the fit kept 205 at **σ = 0.48″** (survey 95th
percentile: 0.38″), and the resulting degree-2 coefficients are ~10× those of
the neighbouring tiles. The polynomial is well constrained where the tie
points are and extrapolates wrongly everywhere else. Real stars there land
~7″ from their true positions, clear the 5″ Gaia/PS1(/USNO-B) vetoes, and
survive as catalogue rows: **85% of the tile's S0 rows have a Gaia star within
12″, 52–53% at 3–8″ (7% by chance), with a coherent offset of (−4.0″, −6.0″).**
`ok` was true throughout.

The tile had been flagged by survivor count in an earlier audit and then
*cleared*, because its refit residual on the tie points looked normal
(0.19–0.33″ median separation to Gaia). That check is blind to exactly this
failure: a fit can be excellent on its tie points and wrong where it has none.

## How much of the catalogue

Every row of both S0 releases was matched against its own tile's plate-epoch
Gaia neighbourhood (`catalogs/gaia_neighbourhood_at_plate.csv`, the
pipeline's own propagated catalogue) within 12″, with the same match on
positions shifted 60″ north as the chance control.

| build | rows | Gaia star at 3–8″ | chance | excess |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `s0-642-20260814` | 122,820 | 7,126 (5.8%) | 15,297 (12.5%) | **−8,171** |
| `s0-642-paper-parity-20260828` | 134,976 | 9,674 (7.2%) | 17,279 (12.8%) | **−7,605** |

**Survey-wide, both catalogues sit well below chance for a nearby Gaia star**
— vetoed candidates avoid stars, as they should. The defect is local:

| tile | plate | rows (08-14 / paper-parity) | at 3–8″ | chance | σ | tie points |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `tile_RA24.686_DECp33.529` | XE296 | 291 / 259 | 52% / 53% | 7% | 0.48″ | 312 |
| `tile_RA63.809_DECp57.375` | XE084 | 46 / 49 | 91% / 92% | 41% / 39% | 0.88″ | 5,812 |

About 130 (120) displaced rows on the first tile and about 20 (25) on the
second — ~0.1% of either catalogue. All 31,458 tiles report `ok`; tie points
run p1 928 / median 3,100; σ p1 0.084″ / median 0.131″ / p95 0.379″ / p99
0.685″. Tiles with a poor fit by those diagnostics (σ > 0.3″ or < 500 tie
points: 2,682 tiles, 8.5%) carry *fewer* survivors than healthy tiles (median
1 vs 3), so a poor fit does not generally inflate the catalogue.

Two things the sweep also showed. Below about ten rows a per-tile
"displaced" criterion is tripped by one or two chance neighbours, so per-tile
figures are only meaningful for tiles with ≥10 rows. And **σ alone would not
have caught the class**: a 13-row tile on XE449 shows the displaced signature
(38% at 3–8″ vs 8%) at σ = 0.15″ on 15,060 tie points.

## How to find such a tile

For each tile with survivors: match the survivors to the tile's
`gaia_neighbourhood_at_plate.csv` within 12″; record the fraction with a
counterpart at 3–8″ and the median offset vector; repeat with the survivors
shifted 60″ in declination for the chance level. A tile whose 3–8″ fraction
exceeds chance by more than ~25 points **and** whose median offset vector is
coherent (|v| > 3″) on ≥10 rows is displaced. The Gaia-invariant check shipped
with the releases (`tools/check_s0_gaia_invariant.py`) tests *within 5″* and
cannot see this.

## What was done about the releases

- Both S0 READMEs carry an appendix naming the two tiles and the counts; the
  data files are unchanged (the releases cannot be regenerated byte for byte)
  and the rows can be dropped by `tile_id`.
- S1 (`results/s1-642-paper-parity-edge15-20260908/`) was re-cut on
  2026-09-09 without the two tiles (revision 2, 95,826 rows; 140 rows removed
  beyond the edge cut). `scripts/stage_edge_post_v2.py --exclude-tiles`
  records the exclusion per tile in the ledger and writes the excluded rows
  to `stage_<STAGE>_EDGE2_excluded.csv`; with no list its outputs are
  byte-identical to before.

## The guard (implementation in progress)

Three layers, because the failure is not visible in any single diagnostic:

1. **Fit-support gates** in `ensure_wcsfix_catalog`: fewer than 500 tie
   points → fit degree 1 instead of 2; σ > 0.4″ at degree 2 → retry at
   degree 1; degree-1 σ > 0.6″ → `ok: false`, reason `unsupported fit`. Raw
   plate coordinates are **not** a safe fallback here — on XE296's corner the
   raw solution is what failed — so an unsupported fit marks the tile for
   quarantine rather than degrading silently.
2. **Post-fit self-check**: after applying the fit, match all corrected
   detections to the tile's plate-epoch Gaia neighbourhood; record the
   fraction with a counterpart at 3–8″ minus the same at a 60″ shift. An
   excess above a calibrated threshold sets `wcsfix_suspect: true` in
   `wcsfix_status.json` and `MNRAS_SUMMARY.json`.
3. **Quarantine at the S0 build**: `scripts/build_run_stage_csvs.py` drops
   rows from suspect or unsupported tiles, counts them in `tile_manifest.csv`
   and lists them in `quarantined_tiles.csv`. On by default;
   `VASCO_WCSFIX_QUARANTINE=0` disables it.

With the guard off, step4 output is byte-identical to today's; with it on,
healthy tiles are byte-identical and only flagged tiles change. Thresholds,
validation on the XE296 and XE084 tiles plus healthy controls, and the
resulting change in S0 counts will be recorded here when the implementation
lands.
